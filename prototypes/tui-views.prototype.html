What views and navigation does the TUI have?

Three structurally different TUIs over the same live mock factory: tasks keep running, the scheduler starts queued work, and attention requests arrive while you look. Click the terminal to focus it, then drive it with the keyboard only (Ink has no mouse). Flip variants with the yellow bar or shift+←/→. The conversation and handoff screens are stubs here; the handoff prototype settled them. Steal bits across variants: "B's home with C's gauges" is a fine answer.
